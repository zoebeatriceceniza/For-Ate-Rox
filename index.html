Dear Ate Roxette,

I am so incredibly happy that you’re finally getting married! Grabe, ate, ang bilis ng panahon. Parang kailan lang natin pinag-uusapan ang plans, preparations, at invitations, tapos bukas na pala ang pinaka-special na araw para sa inyo ni Kuya Maya. At the same time, nalulungkot din ako na hindi ako makakaattend ng wedding mo, especially because you are one of the most important people in my life. I would have loved to witness this beautiful milestone in person and celebrate it with you.

I just want to express how much you mean to me, Ate. You have always been more than just a cousin, you became my sister. You witnessed me grow up through different stages of my life, and I was also blessed to witness your journey into the amazing woman you are today. Through the years, you became one of my greatest inspirations not only in life but also in the career path that I dream of pursuing. Even as I write this letter, I still can’t believe that you’re getting married already.

Thank you for all the memories, guidance, and kindness you have shared with me. Thank you for being someone I could always look up to and admire. I am proud of everything you have achieved and even happier knowing that you have found a partner who will walk beside you through this new chapter of your life.

As you and Kuya Maya begin this journey together, I pray that your marriage will always be filled with love, understanding, patience, laughter, and unwavering support for one another. May your home be blessed with peace and happiness, and may God continue to guide, protect, and strengthen your relationship through every season of life.

Congratulations, Ate Roxette and Kuya Maya. I may not be there physically on your special day, but please know that I will be celebrating with you in spirit and praying for your happiness always.

I love you, Ate Rox. Congratulations once again, and best wishes for a beautiful and blessed married life ahead.

With love,

Zoe
